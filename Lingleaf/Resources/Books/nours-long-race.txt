Nour is fourteen. She lives in Cairo with her family. She is on the school running team. She loves running. But she is not very fast.

Every Tuesday, the team runs on the school track. The coach, Ms. Fahmy, has a big watch. Today the girls are running a short race. It is one hundred meters.

Nour runs very hard. Her friend Salma is first. Nour is last. She is always last.

"Good try, Nour," says Ms. Fahmy.

Nour sits on the grass. She is not happy. She can't run fast. Her legs are long, but they are slow.

After practice, she walks home. Her brother Youssef is at the door.

"Why are you sad?" he asks.

"I am the slowest on the team," says Nour.

"But you never stop," says Youssef. "Remember our walks with Grandpa? You walk for hours. I can't do that!"

Nour thinks about this. She can walk for hours. She can run for a long time. She doesn't need to be fast.

On Thursday, she talks to Ms. Fahmy. "Can I try the long race, please?" she asks. "The three thousand meters."

Ms. Fahmy smiles. "Yes," she says. "Try it on Saturday. There is a big race at the stadium."

On Saturday, the stadium is full. Nour's family is sitting near the track. Youssef has a big sign. It says: "GO, NOUR!"

The race starts. The other girls are running very fast. Nour is not fast. She is last again. But she doesn't stop.

The track is four hundred meters. Nour goes around it once, two times, five times. Now the other girls are tired. Nour is not tired. She is passing one girl. Then she is passing another girl.

"Look!" says Youssef. "She is moving up!"

Now it is the last time around. Nour is second. A tall girl in red is first. Nour runs and runs. She can see the finish line. She can hear her family.

The tall girl wins. Nour is second. She is very happy.

Ms. Fahmy runs to her. "Second place!" she says. "Nour, you are not slow. You are strong."

"Was I good?" asks Nour.

"You were great," says Ms. Fahmy. "This is your race."

That evening, Nour puts her medal on the wall. Youssef puts his sign next to it.

"Next time, you are first," he says.

Nour laughs. "Next time, you run with me," she says.
