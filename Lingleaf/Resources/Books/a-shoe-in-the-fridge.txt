Diego lives in Lima with his two children. Sofía is six years old. Mateo is four years old. Their mom, Carla, is a nurse. Today she is working early.

Every morning, Diego takes the children to the bus. The school bus comes at eight. Now it is seven thirty. Diego is making breakfast. The kitchen is very busy.

"Sofía, eat your eggs, please," says Diego.

"I don't like eggs today," says Sofía.

"You like eggs every day," says Diego. "Eat them, please."

Mateo is under the table. He is singing a song. He has a shoe on one foot. The other foot has only a sock.

"Mateo, where is your other shoe?" asks Diego.

"I don't know," says Mateo. He has a big smile.

Diego looks in the bedroom. He looks under the bed. He looks in the bathroom. There is no shoe there. He looks at the clock. It is seven forty.

"Help me, Sofía," he says. "Look for Mateo's shoe."

Sofía looks in the living room. She looks behind the sofa. There is a toy car and an old banana. There is no shoe.

Diego is getting nervous. Then his phone is ringing. It is Carla.

"Hi," says Carla. "How is your morning?"

"Not good," says Diego. "Mateo has only one shoe. The bus comes in fifteen minutes."

Carla laughs. "Look in the fridge," she says.

"The fridge?" says Diego.

"Yes. Yesterday, Mateo was a shopkeeper. His shop was in the fridge."

Diego opens the fridge. There is milk. There is cheese. There is a blue shoe next to the eggs. The shoe is very cold.

"Mateo!" says Diego. "Your shoe is in the fridge!"

"Yes," says Mateo. "It is for sale."

Diego laughs very hard. He can't stop laughing. Sofía is laughing too.

Diego puts the shoe on Mateo's foot. "Come on," he says. "Get your bags. Go, go, go!"

They run out of the house. The bus is waiting at the corner. The driver, Mrs. Quispe, is smiling.

"Good morning!" she says. "You are just in time."

The children get on the bus. Mateo waves from the window. His foot is cold, but he is happy.

Diego goes back to the kitchen. He sits down at the table. He eats Sofía's eggs. They are cold too.

That evening, Carla comes home. She opens the fridge. There is a note on the milk. It says: "Shop closed. No shoes, please!"
