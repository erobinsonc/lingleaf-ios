Efua is twenty-two. She has a new job in Accra. Today, she has a new apartment too. It is her first apartment.

The apartment is on the third floor. It has one bedroom and a small kitchen. The bathroom has a blue door. The living room has a big window. From the window, she can see the city.

But the apartment is empty. There is no bed. There are no chairs. She has no table.

Her bag is on the floor. A box of books is next to it. That is all.

Efua calls her brother, Kojo. "Hi, Kojo," she says. "I am in my new apartment."

"Great!" says Kojo. "Is it nice?"

"It is nice," she says. "But it is empty. I have no bed. Tonight, I'm sleeping on the floor."

"Don't worry," says Kojo. "Wait for me. I have an idea."

Efua sits on the floor. She eats a sandwich. She drinks water from a bottle. She feels a little sad.

Then somebody knocks on the door. It is a woman with a big smile. She has a green plant in her hands.

"Hello! I am Ama," says the woman. "I live next door. Welcome to the building!"

"Thank you," says Efua. "Please come in. But there are no chairs!"

Ama laughs. She looks around the room. "Wait here," she says. "Don't go away."

Ten minutes later, Ama comes back. She has two old chairs. A young man is with her. He has a small table.

"This is my son, Kwesi," says Ama. "We have extra things. Take them, please."

Efua is very happy. Now there are two chairs and a table. They sit and drink tea.

Ama tells her about the building. There is a market on the corner. There is a good bus to the city center. The man on the first floor fixes phones.

In the evening, Kojo arrives. He has a mattress on his car. His friend is with him. They carry it up the stairs. It is very heavy.

"Here is your bed," says Kojo. "It was my bed in college."

"Thank you!" says Efua.

Now the apartment is not empty. There is a bed and a table. There are two chairs and a green plant. Four people are in the small kitchen. They are eating rice and beans.

"Your apartment is great," says Kojo.

Efua smiles. "Yes," she says. "My neighbors are great too."
